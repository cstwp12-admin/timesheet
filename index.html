HQ submissionFor the compiler: two steps each month

  1. Each person fills in the month and taps Print / PDF → Show QR.
  2. Team timesheets: scan the QRs, the RE signs if digital, Print all.
  3. Stamps and signatures by hand, SOR last; scan the stack on the photocopier.
  4. Signed copies for HQ: add the scan PDF, the names are read from the pages, Check pages, Make HQ PDF.

1 · Team timesheetsCollect, sign, print

Each person opens Print / PDF → Show QR and you scan it (a printed timesheet's QR works too). The RE can sign digitally under Verified by PYKP. Then print all in one go for stamps and signatures, keep one Excel workbook (a sheet per person) or get a summary.

2 · Signed copies for HQOne PDF with a cover page for submission to HQ

After the SOR has signed, scan the whole stack on the photocopier into one PDF and add it here (or scan sheet by sheet). The app reads the printed name on each page and matches it to the team list (pages it cannot read follow the print order); then Check pages to confirm. The cover page lists whose timesheet is on which page.
